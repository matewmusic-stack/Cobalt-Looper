Internal
120 BPM
Signal path: unarmed track → main (unchanged). Armed track → Cue insert (mix of incoming audio and its loop, set by Cue Vol) → main. Starts and stops land on bar lines of one shared sample counter, so every loop is a whole number of bars and stays aligned. Internal tempo is locked while a loop exists. With External clock the grid follows incoming MIDI clock (24 ppqn): Start restarts at bar 1, Stop holds everything, Continue resumes. Loop lengths stay fixed in samples, so a large tempo change after recording will drift.
